# Clubs and cards

## Connect your website account

Open **`MANAGE-CLUBS.cmd`** and choose **Connect**. The picker displays a short code and opens the corresponding approval page on [wccf.online](https://wccf.online). Sign in and approve the matching code.

Select a club, then choose **View profile**, **Play Satellite** or **Open Terminal**. Create additional clubs with **New club on website**. An existing installation can use **Link current club** to link that club to the website account.

Your clubs use separate local game profiles, Aime identities and printer counters. Close the game before changing clubs. Do not merge those folders or copy one club's credentials into another.

## Play and manage your cards

Use **Play Satellite** for tutorials and matches. The formation panel shows the usable cards for the selected club: drag cards onto the pitch, move them, and drag them back to the available cards to take them off. **Reset to saved lineup** restores the saved arrangement.

Use **Open Terminal** for the Digital Card List, scouting and club options. Use its Sort, Asc./Desc., Filter and Reset controls to browse your cards.

A Digital Card List can be empty when you have no unprinted digital cards, even though printed cards appear in your playable collection. Scouting adds digital cards; printing and rank-up change their state.

Rank-up keeps the old card as **Evolved** and creates its replacement. The retired original is hidden from the usable formation panel. Refreshing through the next game session loads the playable replacement; this does not mean the old ownership record was deleted.

Scout tickets are spent by scouting. A stock of zero after using them is expected. The client does not create or refund tickets to make a screen appear populated.

## Save and close

Let the game finish saving and log out normally before closing its window. Keep the whole installation and its per-club profiles; those folders contain local print history, protected credentials and information needed to resume safely.

Website account progress does not make every local file disposable. Do not delete an installation as a troubleshooting shortcut. Windows protects credentials for the Windows user who created them; copying protected files to a different PC or Windows account is not a supported sign-in method. Use the club picker's normal website connection on another PC.

## Updates

Updates are applied while the game is closed. The client verifies signed release metadata, archive size and hashes before installing it, and keeps the previous release for recovery. It preserves player state during updates.

Do not replace `trusted-public-key.bin` to silence an update error. Use the [troubleshooting guide](troubleshooting.md) and report the exact message.
